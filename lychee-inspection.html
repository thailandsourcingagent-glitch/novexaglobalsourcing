Inspection Summary CONFIRMED

  • Batch size distribution measured in the 30–32mm range using a standard sizing gauge.
  • Sample weights consistent at ~0.34–0.36 kg per lot.
  • Brix (sweetness) readings ranged 15.4%–15.9%, indicating consistent sugar content across samples.
  • Flesh-to-seed ratio visually confirmed via cross-section sampling.
  • Fruit sorted into three quality categories consistent with client grading requirements.
