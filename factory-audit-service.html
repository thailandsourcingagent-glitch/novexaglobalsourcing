Introduction

What Is a Factory Audit?

A factory audit is an on-site visit to a supplier's actual production facility, carried out before you place a significant order or sign a supply agreement. Our inspector confirms the factory physically exists at the stated address, verifies its business registration, and assesses whether it genuinely has the capacity, machinery, and workforce to produce your order.

It's the difference between trusting a supplier's photos and website, and having someone independent walk the production floor on your behalf.

Why This Service Matters

Not Every "Factory" Is a Factory

Trading companies sometimes present themselves as manufacturers. Photos on a supplier's website can be years old, or belong to a different facility entirely. A factory audit closes that gap before your money is committed.

  • ✕ Placing a large order with a trading company posing as a factory
  • ✕ Discovering the factory can't actually meet your required capacity
  • ✕ A supplier whose business license doesn't match who you're paying
  • ✕ No independent record of the facility before committing tooling costs
  • ✕ Compliance risk from unverified labor or safety conditions

FAQ

Common Questions

When should I request a factory audit? +
Before placing a first order with a new supplier, especially for larger orders, custom tooling, or long-term supply agreements.
Will the factory know an audit is happening? +
Audits can be announced (scheduled with the factory in advance) or unannounced, depending on what you need. We'll advise which fits your situation.
What if the audit finds problems? +
You receive a clear report of what was found. You then decide whether to proceed, request changes, or look at alternative suppliers — the decision stays with you.
Do you audit factories you didn't find for us? +
Yes. Factory audits are available as a standalone service for any supplier you're considering, whether we sourced them or not.
Is a factory audit the same as a Pre-Shipment Inspection? +
No. A factory audit verifies the supplier itself before you order. A Pre-Shipment Inspection checks the actual goods after production, before they ship. Many clients use both at different stages.
