Introduction

What Is a Container Loading Check?

A Container Loading Check (CLC) is on-site supervision of the actual loading of your goods into the shipping container — from the moment the empty container arrives at the factory to the moment it's sealed and ready to depart.

Our inspector is physically present throughout the process, verifying the container itself, counting cartons as they're loaded, checking stacking and space utilization, and recording the seal number applied at the end. It's the closest thing to being there yourself, without needing to fly out for every shipment.

Why This Service Matters

What Happens Behind Closed Container Doors

Once the doors close and the seal goes on, you have no way to check what actually made it into the container — unless someone was there to see it happen.

  • ✕ Short-shipped cartons discovered only after the container arrives
  • ✕ Goods loaded into a dirty, damp, or damaged container
  • ✕ Poor stacking causing crushed cartons or shifted cargo in transit
  • ✕ No independent record of the seal number applied
  • ✕ Disputes over what was loaded, with no evidence either way

FAQ

Common Questions

When should I book a CLC? +
As soon as your loading date is confirmed with the factory or forwarder — ideally with at least 24–48 hours' notice so we can schedule an inspector.
Can CLC be combined with a Pre-Shipment Inspection? +
Yes — many clients schedule a PSI a day or two before loading, then have the same inspector return for the CLC once the container arrives.
What if the container fails the empty-container check? +
Loading is paused and you're notified immediately so a replacement container can be arranged before your goods go in.
Do you supervise LCL (less-than-container) loads too? +
Yes, CLC supervision is available for both full container loads (FCL) and consolidated (LCL) shipments.
Will I get the seal number the same day? +
Yes — the seal number is recorded on-site and included in your report, typically sent within 24 hours of loading.
