Introduction

What Is a During Production Inspection (DPI)?

A DPI happens once production is underway — typically when 20–80% of the order is complete. Unlike a Pre-Shipment Inspection, which checks finished, packed goods, a DPI catches issues while there's still time to correct the production line before the rest of the order is made.

Why This Service Matters

Fix It on Unit 200, Not Unit 2,000

A defect found at 100% completion means the whole order is affected. Found at 30%, it's a quick production-line fix.

  • ✕ A material or color issue discovered only after full production
  • ✕ No visibility into progress until the goods are already packed
  • ✕ Small line issues compounding across thousands of units
  • ✕ Missed production deadlines discovered too late to adjust

FAQ

Common Questions

How is this different from a Pre-Shipment Inspection? +
A DPI happens while production is still running, so issues can be corrected before more units are made. A PSI happens after production is finished and goods are packed, checking the final result.
When should I schedule a DPI? +
Typically once production is 20–80% complete — early enough to fix issues, late enough to see representative output.
Can I combine DPI and PSI on the same order? +
Yes — many clients schedule a DPI mid-production, then a PSI once goods are packed and ready to ship, for full coverage.
