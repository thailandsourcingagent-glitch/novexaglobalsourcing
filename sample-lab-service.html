Introduction

What This Service Covers

For products that need lab-verified data — food safety, material composition, purity, or regulatory compliance — we collect samples on-site under controlled conditions and hand them directly to an accredited laboratory, keeping the chain of custody independent from the supplier.

Why This Service Matters

Supplier-Provided Test Reports Aren't Always Independent

A test report is only as trustworthy as the sample it was based on. Independent collection removes the question of whether the tested sample actually represents your order.

  • ✕ A "best case" sample submitted by the supplier for testing
  • ✕ No chain of custody between factory and laboratory
  • ✕ Compliance certificates that don't match the actual batch shipped
  • ✕ Delays finding a qualified lab for a specific test type

FAQ

Common Questions

What kinds of tests can be arranged? +
Depending on your product, this can include moisture content, fat/sugar composition, material purity, safety compliance, and other lab-verifiable parameters. Tell us your requirement and we'll match you with a suitable lab.
How long does testing take? +
Sample collection is typically same-day. Lab turnaround varies by test type, usually a few days to two weeks.
Can this be combined with a PSI or factory audit? +
Yes — sample collection is often scheduled alongside a factory visit or pre-shipment inspection to save time and cost.
