Introduction

What Is Supplier Verification?

Supplier verification confirms that the company you're about to pay is a real, legally registered business — not a shell company, a scam listing, or a trading company misrepresenting itself as a manufacturer. It's a focused check on the business entity itself, separate from a full factory audit.

Why This Service Matters

Deposits Sent to Unverified Suppliers Rarely Come Back

Fraudulent listings and misrepresented companies exist on every major B2B platform. A short verification check before you pay is far cheaper than recovering a lost deposit.

  • ✕ Paying a deposit to a company that doesn't legally exist
  • ✕ Bank details that don't match the registered business name
  • ✕ A "manufacturer" that's actually a trading company
  • ✕ No way to verify claims made in a sales pitch

FAQ

Common Questions

Is this the same as a factory audit? +
No. Supplier verification checks the business entity's legitimacy. A factory audit goes further, physically visiting the production facility to assess capacity and capability.
How fast can this be done? +
Most verifications are completed within 48–72 hours of receiving the supplier's details.
What if the supplier fails verification? +
You receive a clear explanation of the discrepancy found, so you can decide how to proceed before sending any payment.
